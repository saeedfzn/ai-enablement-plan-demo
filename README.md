# My first 90 days as AI Enablement Manager (concept demo)

**Live demo:** https://saeedfzn.github.io/ai-enablement-plan-demo/

A one-page, clickable plan for rolling out AI skills across a fictional 400-person accountancy and advisory firm. Each section maps to one part of an AI Enablement Manager role:

- **Assess usage:** a seven-question baseline survey you can take as a sample employee. It gives a profile (Explorer, Practitioner or Champion), flags unsafe answers on client data, unapproved tools and checking (any flag caps the profile below Champion), and sits next to made-up results from 120 colleagues by team, with a table view.
- **Training by role:** learning paths for partners, associates, PAs/business services and IT, each with learning objectives, confidentiality rules, prompt patterns, a hands-on exercise, evidence that it worked and the time needed.
- **Learning materials:** a searchable, filterable prompt library with "what to check" and a data-sensitivity rating on every card.
- **Responsible use:** client confidentiality, privilege, professional judgement, checking outputs, personal data (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and regulator expectations, kept general, plus four quick scenarios.
- **Stakeholders:** a 30/60/90-day plan with milestones, who is involved and success measures, filterable by stakeholder.
- **Adoption and feedback:** weekly active users, use cases adopted, confidence before and after, the unsafe client-data answer re-measured at day 90, and a "you said, we did" log, filterable by team.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file, with no build step and no tracking. Everything is made up: the firm, people, responses and figures. It shows how I would approach the role, not a programme I have run; the page says plainly what I have and haven't done. The responsible-use notes are general principles, not legal or regulatory advice.

It's a companion to two earlier demos: an [AI safety training module](https://saeedfzn.github.io/ai-at-work-training-demo/) (the foundation step in each learning path) and an [AI usage and governance dashboard](https://saeedfzn.github.io/ai-usage-governance-demo/).
